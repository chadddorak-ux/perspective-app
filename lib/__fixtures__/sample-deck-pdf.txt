About us 
Bayside Girls Who Build is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Oakland, CA that runs free robotics and engineering clubs for
girls in grades 6–12. This year 400 students across 18 schools are building robots for Build Day 2027, our annual
STEM showcase and robotics competition.
Build Day brings 1,200 students, families and teachers to the Oakland Convention Center for a day of robot
matches, hands-on engineering workshops and a college and careers fair. Most of our students come from low-
income families and will be the first in their family to go to college. 
Who you'll reach 
1,200 attendees: girls in middle school and high school, their families and teachers
Local press and 8,000 newsletter subscribers
