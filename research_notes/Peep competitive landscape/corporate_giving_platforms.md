# Corporate Giving, CSR and Employee-Giving Platforms (Brand/Company Side of Nonprofit Funding)

Research date: 2026-10-23. US focus. Note on method: several vendor sites (bonterratech.com, globenewswire, benevity help center) blocked the fetch tool, so some pages were retrieved by direct download and some facts come only from search-result summaries; those are flagged. Vendor comparison pages written by competitors (e.g., Uncommon Giving about YourCause/Bonterra) are cited but should be treated as biased.

## Q1. Who are the major players, what do they do, who do they serve, and how are they priced?

### Takeaway
The category is dominated by a few enterprise suites (Benevity, Bonterra [CyberGrants + Deed], Blackbaud YourCause) priced at roughly $30K-$150K+/yr in quote-only deals, plus a tier of mid-market/SMB tools (Groundswell, Millie, Uncommon Giving, Givinga, Submittable/Bright Funds, Goodstack) with published or lower pricing. Almost all are built around *employee* engagement (workplace giving, matching, volunteering, ERGs) and *grant administration*; none are built around a brand discovering and funding specific nonprofit campaigns as a marketing/sponsorship decision.

## Q2. How do companies find and vet nonprofits, and do any support sponsorship of events/campaigns with benefits/tiers?

### Takeaway
Discovery is a giant searchable database of pre-vetted registered charities (1.8M-10M+ orgs), with vetting = legal status/eligibility/compliance checks and bank verification. Nonprofits can post profiles and "fundraising projects," but there is no standardized campaign format, no sponsorship packages/benefit tiers, and no brand-facing marketplace for buying sponsorships; that lives in separate, mostly event/sports-oriented sponsorship marketplaces.

### Cited Findings
- Benevity: nonprofits self-register or are nominated by a corporate client; Benevity performs an "eligibility and compliance review"; nonprofits then build a profile (logo, mission, tags) and post "fundraising projects," volunteer needs, and grant applications via the Causes Portal — [Benevity for Nonprofits](https://benevity.com/nonprofits). The 2026 Causes Portal update gives nonprofits "improved visibility into their funding pipeline and consolidated reporting" — [Benevity Live! 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/benevity-unveils-ai-native-csr-134000816.html)
- Benevity lists "Nonprofit Due Diligence — continuously validated" as a solution — [Benevity site nav](https://benevity.com/press-releases/the-benevity-state-of-corporate-purpose-2026-report-finds-corporate-giving-is-up-eight-points-as-companies-face-socio-political-pressure)
- Nonprofits report friction in Benevity bank verification (repeated EFT rejection) — [Capterra review, Aug 2024](https://www.capterra.com/p/155271/Benevity/reviews/)
- YourCause: "Blackbaud Verified Network" proprietary nonprofit intelligence; upcoming Intelligent Search and personalized recommendations to "surface opportunities based on desired impact rather than requiring users to search for a specific organization" — [Blackbaud May 2026](https://www.blackbaud.com/newsroom/article/pubs-may-2026)
- Bonterra Network "powers grant scoring, applicant matching, and nonprofit discovery" from 640M+ transactions — [Bonterra Deed launch](https://www.bonterratech.com/news-press/bonterra-launches-deed)
- Goodstack sells nonprofit verification as an API product (e.g., for SaaS nonprofit discounts) — [Goodstack](https://goodstack.io/)
- Submittable Give pre-loads 3,000 nonprofits vetted by GlobalGiving — [NonProfit PRO](https://www.nonprofitpro.com/article/submittables-new-give-platform-helps-companies-meet-employee-demand-for-corporate-giving/)
- Uncommon Giving: 1.8M+ vetted 501(c)(3)s with EIN deduping across grants and employee giving — [Uncommon Giving vs Bonterra](https://uncommongiving.com/comparison/bonterra/)
- Sponsorship: dedicated sponsorship-request intake products exist but are old/niche (FrontStream launched sponsorship request management in 2016) — [Newswire](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/frontstream-announces-the-launch-of-a-new-sponsorship-request-management-product-569093541.html). Brand-side sponsorship marketplaces are generalist and sports/festival-oriented: Anvara — [Anvara](https://www.anvara.com/); SponsorFlo (free to browse/post, includes nonprofits) — [SponsorFlo](https://app.sponsorflo.ai/marketplace); SponsorMyEvent — [SponsorMyEvent](https://sponsormyevent.com/). Nonprofits are advised to use Double the Donation's corporate sponsorship database for prospect research — [Double the Donation](https://doublethedonation.com/corporate-sponsorship-research-playbook/)
- Givsum offers "Sponsorships" as a nonprofit-side event fundraising feature — [Givsum pricing](https://www.givsum.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- Vetting in the category is about *legal eligibility and payment safety* (is it a registered charity, is the bank account real, is it on a sanctions list), not about *campaign quality* — nothing standardizes what a nonprofit's ask looks like, budget, outcomes, or sponsor benefits.
- Corporate sponsorship (events, galas, programs with logo placement/benefit tiers) sits outside CSR software: typically handled by marketing via email/PDF decks or through generic sponsorship marketplaces. This is a clear white space for a standardized one-pager + tiers model.
- I found no CSR platform product page describing sponsorship packages with benefit tiers; the only "sponsorship" tooling is request-intake forms or nonprofit-side event tools.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether CyberGrants or GrantsConnect currently ship a dedicated "sponsorship request" workflow (likely configurable via grant forms; no source found).
- Exact Benevity/YourCause eligibility exclusions (religious, political orgs) were not retrieved from primary docs.

## Q3. Does any platform automatically match a company's giving budget/interests to specific campaigns?

### Takeaway
Not really. AI in this category (2025-2026) is aimed at administrator efficiency (grant summaries/scoring, match verification, agentic admin workspaces) and at personalized *employee* recommendations. No major CSR suite automatically allocates a company's budget across matched nonprofit campaigns. The closest analog is a boutique agency tool (Hello Good's GoodMatch) for brand-nonprofit cause-marketing partnerships.

### Cited Findings
- Benevity: AI Grant Summaries (Mar 2026; 30%+ of grantmaking clients used within a week); AI Grants Scoring using program rubric; "Match Assurance" reads employee receipts and auto-submits matches (adopted by 500+ clients; external match decline rates down from 30% to <3%); "Impact Workspace" agentic product "coming later this year" (2026) — [Benevity grantmaking release](https://benevity.com/press-releases/global-grantmaking-ai-management-features), [Benevity Live! 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/benevity-unveils-ai-native-csr-134000816.html)
- YourCause: gen-AI chat to create engagement elements/volunteer events; AI Grant Summaries; upcoming intelligent search and personalized recommendations for employees based on activity and cause interests — [Blackbaud May 2026](https://www.blackbaud.com/newsroom/article/pubs-may-2026)
- Bonterra Deed: "applicant matching" and nonprofit discovery from network data; Deed's "ImpactAI" suite automates manual tasks — [Bonterra Deed launch](https://www.bonterratech.com/news-press/bonterra-launches-deed), [Bonterra acquires Deed](https://www.bonterratech.com/news-press/bonterra-acquires-deed)
- Groundswell and Uncommon Giving: AI-powered grant scoring / AI-drafted applications — [Groundswell pricing](https://groundswell.io/pricing), [Uncommon Giving vs Bonterra](https://uncommongiving.com/comparison/bonterra/)
- Hello Good "GoodMatch" (Feb 2026): AI scores brand-nonprofit partnerships on cause alignment, audience overlap, geography, budget fit, values; humans make warm intros; claims 3x more partnership conversations, 2x faster close (self-reported) — [Hello Good](https://hellogood.club/news/ai-cause-marketing)
- Selflessly positions as "AI-native corporate giving" for 75-2,500-employee companies, with an AI assistant for employees — [Selflessly LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/selflessly) (company page; launch date unclear)
- Industry sentiment: 87% of corporate impact leaders think AI will reduce nonprofit burden, but only 16% say AI is core to their impact strategy; concerns include reinforcing funding biases (77%), excluding smaller orgs (75%), misclassifying sensitive-mission nonprofits (60%) — [Benevity State of Corporate Purpose 2026](https://benevity.com/press-releases/the-benevity-state-of-corporate-purpose-2026-report-finds-corporate-giving-is-up-eight-points-as-companies-face-socio-political-pressure)

### Inferences
- "Set budget + pick causes → get matched to campaigns → we move the money" is not a workflow any incumbent sells to brands; incumbents' grant tools assume an inbound RFP/application cycle that the company designs and staffs.
- Buyer hesitation about AI bias/excluding small orgs is a design requirement for any Peep matching algorithm (explainable matches, small-org inclusion).

### Gaps
- No evidence found of any funded startup (2024-2026) whose core product is AI matching of corporate budgets to nonprofit campaigns; search coverage may be incomplete.

## Q4. What happens after the gift (impact reporting, tax receipts, communication, recognition/brand assets)?

### Takeaway
Post-gift tooling serves the company's internal reporting (participation, dollars, hours, SROI dashboards) and employee tax receipts. Payments to nonprofits are often slow and opaque, nonprofit reporting burden is rising, and there's no structured channel for brand-nonprofit relationship, recognition, or co-marketing assets.

### Cited Findings
- Reporting is internal and leadership-facing: Bonterra "Unified Impact View" for real-time leadership reporting — [Bonterra Deed launch](https://www.bonterratech.com/news-press/bonterra-launches-deed); Millie "C-suite ready metrics" — [Millie](https://www.getmillie.com/); Groundswell unified reporting across grants, giving, volunteering, plus post-award agreements and grantee reporting templates — [Groundswell pricing](https://groundswell.io/pricing)
- Tax receipts are employee-facing: Uncommon Giving auto-generates January tax receipts from employee DAF accounts — [Uncommon Giving vs YourCause](https://uncommongiving.com/comparison/yourcause/); Groundswell "tax receipts in one place" — [Groundswell pricing](https://groundswell.io/pricing); Pledge lists tax receipts — [Pledge pricing](https://www.pledge.to/pricing)
- Disbursement speed: YourCause standard 20-60 days; Expedited Giving pilot for select US nonprofits claims 90-95% faster — [Uncommon Giving vs YourCause](https://uncommongiving.com/comparison/yourcause/); Blackbaud says Expedited Giving will deliver funds "in as little as two days" later in 2026, plus a new Processing Portal for admin visibility over funds — [Blackbaud May 2026](https://www.blackbaud.com/newsroom/article/pubs-may-2026). Benevity reviewers report up to two months for disbursement — [G2 (via search summary)](https://www.g2.com/products/benevity/reviews)
- Donor-side traceability complaints: "I have no independent confirmation that the ACS actually received my donation"; "no way to track on the UI on fund arrival date" — [Capterra Benevity reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/155271/Benevity/reviews/)
- Nonprofit burden: last year 70% of companies planned to reduce nonprofit reporting requirements; now only 10% expect to; nearly half of nonprofits absorb added workload via unpaid overtime; 49% say corporate donors rarely/never fund it — [Benevity State of Corporate Purpose 2026](https://benevity.com/press-releases/the-benevity-state-of-corporate-purpose-2026-report-finds-corporate-giving-is-up-eight-points-as-companies-face-socio-political-pressure)
- Corporate motive is reputation: top reason companies invest in purpose is reputation and trust; communications and storytelling went from 9th-ranked investment priority (2024) to second fastest-growing budget line (2026) — [Benevity State of Corporate Purpose 2026](https://benevity.com/press-releases/the-benevity-state-of-corporate-purpose-2026-report-finds-corporate-giving-is-up-eight-points-as-companies-face-socio-political-pressure)
- Benevity markets itself to nonprofits as "your marketing platform to reach new supporters" via profiles and projects, i.e., discovery by employees, not post-gift relationship with brands — [Benevity for Nonprofits](https://benevity.com/nonprofits)

### Inferences
- Incumbent "verification that funds arrived" is a back-office payment status, often invisible to the giver; a verifiable, nonprofit-confirmed receipt + proof-of-delivery is a differentiator.
- Since brands increasingly fund giving for reputation/storytelling, the lack of post-gift brand assets (impact story, logo usage rights, co-branded content, recognition) is a meaningful gap — CSR suites report numbers to the board, not stories to the market.
- Optional, lightweight communication (Peep's model) responds to the rising nonprofit reporting burden documented above.

### Gaps
- No source found describing any CSR platform producing nonprofit-to-brand recognition packages, sponsor logo kits, or co-marketing assets.

## Q7. Cross-cutting: what this category does NOT do for brands

### Takeaway
CSR/employee-giving software is an HR-and-compliance system of record for employee giving and inbound grantmaking. It does not provide brand-led discovery of standardized nonprofit campaigns, sponsorship packaging, budget-to-campaign matching, verified proof-of-delivery visible to the funder, or a managed post-gift brand-nonprofit relationship — all the things Peep proposes.

### Cited Findings
- Core value propositions are employee participation and admin efficiency: "Employees do not want another clunky portal" — [Bonterra CEO](https://www.bonterratech.com/news-press/bonterra-acquires-deed); Benevity's AI framing is "taking more of the operational burden off [impact leaders'] plates" — [Benevity Live! 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/benevity-unveils-ai-native-csr-134000816.html)
- Nonprofit content on these platforms is self-authored profiles/"projects" (logo, mission, tags, current needs), not a standardized campaign format — [Benevity for Nonprofits](https://benevity.com/nonprofits)
- Grant tools are application-intake systems (branded application, eligibility screening, rubric scoring, approvals, disbursement, grantee reporting) — [Uncommon Giving vs YourCause](https://uncommongiving.com/comparison/yourcause/), [Groundswell pricing](https://groundswell.io/pricing)
- Sponsorship marketplaces exist separately and skew to sports/festivals/creators — [Anvara](https://www.anvara.com/), [SponsorFlo](https://app.sponsorflo.ai/marketplace)
- Brand-nonprofit partnership matching that does exist is agency-led, not self-serve with payment handling — [Hello Good](https://hellogood.club/news/ai-cause-marketing)
- Communications/storytelling budgets are rising fastest among corporate purpose priorities — [Benevity State of Corporate Purpose 2026](https://benevity.com/press-releases/the-benevity-state-of-corporate-purpose-2026-report-finds-corporate-giving-is-up-eight-points-as-companies-face-socio-political-pressure)

### Inferences
- Specific gaps Peep could fill:
  1. **Discovery of campaigns, not organizations**: incumbents index charities (EINs); none normalize a nonprofit's pitch deck into a comparable, polished campaign page with budget, goal, outcomes and sponsor benefits.
  2. **Budget-first matching for the company itself** (not employees): set an annual budget + cause filters → recommended portfolio of campaigns. Incumbents offer this only as employee recommendations (YourCause, upcoming) or inbound grant scoring.
  3. **Sponsorship with tiers/benefits**: absent from CSR suites; handled ad hoc by marketing.
  4. **SMB economics**: no seat-based SaaS fee; incumbents start at $5K-$50K+/yr before a dollar is given.
  5. **Funder-visible proof of delivery + receipt**: incumbents' disbursement status is opaque and slow (20-60 days typical for YourCause; complaints of months for Benevity).
  6. **Optional, low-burden relationship layer**: reporting burden on nonprofits is rising with unpaid overtime; a structured but optional update/recognition channel (impact story, logo-use, thank-you assets) is unaddressed.
- Likely competitive responses/risks: Benevity and Bonterra already hold the largest vetted-nonprofit networks (2.5M and 10M+ claimed) and payment rails; they could add a "company campaigns" module. Infra players (Chariot, Goodstack, Pledge, Givinga) could be partners for verification/disbursement rather than competitors. Peep must also handle legal structure for issuing tax receipts (incumbents generally route via their own DAF/foundation entity — e.g., Benevity's DAF, Uncommon Giving and Groundswell employee DAFs).

### Gaps
- Did not verify how incumbents legally structure company (vs employee) gifts for tax receipting in each case (DAF intermediary vs direct-to-charity); worth a dedicated legal/structure investigation.
- No CECP Giving in Numbers 2025/2026 data retrieved in this pass.
